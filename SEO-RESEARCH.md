## 6. GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE

### Why It's Critical

Google Business Profile (GBP) is THE most important local SEO factor for Reiki in Berlin:

- The local pack (map results) appears ABOVE organic results for "Reiki Berlin"
- 6 out of 6 visible businesses in the Bing local pack had GBP listings
- For mobile searches (majority of traffic), the local pack takes up the entire first screen
- GBP provides: reviews, photos, hours, booking link, Q&A, posts
- Competitors like Kornelia Haunschild, Romana, and Nina Kim all appear in local packs

### What Kiki Should Set Up

1. **Create Google Business Profile immediately** (even before website launch)
   - Category: "Reiki Therapist" (primary) + "Energy Healing Practitioner" (secondary)
   - Service area: Berlin (or specific Bezirke)
   - Languages: German, English
   - Booking link: direct to Cal.com scheduling

2. **Optimize the profile**:
   - Complete description with keywords (Reiki, Sound Healing, Berlin, English, Fernreiki)
   - Add ALL services with prices
   - Upload 10+ high-quality photos (studio, treatment room, Kiki, instruments)
   - Set accurate business hours
   - Enable messaging
   - Add Q&A (pre-fill with common questions)

3. **Review strategy**:
   - Ask every client to leave a Google review
   - Respond to every review (shows engagement)
   - Aim for 10+ reviews in first 3 months
   - Reviews with keywords ("Reiki", "sound healing", "English") help rankings

4. **Google Business Posts**:
   - Post weekly (events, tips, offers, blog snippets)
   - Use Google Posts for seasonal promotions
   - Include call-to-action buttons
